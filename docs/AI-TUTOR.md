# Ask for help as a learner

Copy this prompt, then add the exercise ID, your prediction, your attempted command, execution context and observed output. Remove secrets first.

```text
Help me understand this Linux security exercise as a tutor.
Ask what I predict before suggesting a test. Ask for my actual attempted
command, execution user/machine and output. Give one hint at a time and
wait for my attempt. Help me explain the mechanism and one limitation.
Do not write a complete submission or fabricate experimental evidence.
When a suggestion changes permissions, authentication or filtering,
help me identify a recovery route and a test of the intended boundary.
```

This prompt is study guidance, not a guarantee of AI behaviour. Check commands and explanations against the course and your disposable lab. A teacher-controlled grading prompt is separate from learner tutoring.
